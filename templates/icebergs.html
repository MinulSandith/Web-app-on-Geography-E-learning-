Icebergs

An iceberg is a piece of freshwater ice more than 15 m long that has broken off a glacier or an ice shelf and is floating freely in open water. Icebergs are generally found in the Arctic and Antarctic oceans.
Because the density of pure ice is about 920 kg/m3 and that of sea water about 1025 kg/m3, typically only one-tenth of the volume of an iceberg is above the water's surface, giving rise to the idiom "tip of the iceberg".
