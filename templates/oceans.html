Oceans

The ocean is the body of salt water that covers approximately 70.8% of the surface of Earth. The ocean is conventionally divided into five large oceanic regions: the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Antarctic (Southern), and Arctic Oceans.
The ocean is the primary component of Earth's hydrosphere, and thus is essential to life on Earth, forms part of the carbon cycle, and influences climate and weather patterns.
